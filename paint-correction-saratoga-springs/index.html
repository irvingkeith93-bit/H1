Before you book

Simple answers to the important questions.

Is paint correction recommended before ceramic coating?

Yes. Most vehicles have at least some light marring, haze, wash marks or other defects. H1 normally recommends correcting the paint to the appropriate level before coating so the protected finish looks its best.

Can I book coating without paint correction?

It is possible when the paint is already correction-ready—such as a properly corrected vehicle or a rare new vehicle with genuinely defect-free paint. H1 will inspect it first. Coating without correction is available, but it is not the usual recommendation.

Does a new vehicle ever need correction?

Often, yes. New vehicles can still have transport marks, dealership wash damage or light defects. Some need very little work, while others need more. The correction scope stays proportional to what the paint actually requires.

Why does Square show a 24-hour service window?

The current Premium Ceramic Coating booking reserves a 24-hour block for every vehicle category. That protects enough time for preparation, correction, coating and initial curing. H1 confirms the exact handoff schedule before the appointment.

Can the starting price change?

Yes. The listed amount is the starting point for that vehicle category. Final pricing depends on paint condition and the correction level approved after inspection, so you know the complete scope before work begins.

Paint correction

Correction before protection.

Paint correction is the controlled machine-polishing process used to improve defects in a vehicle's clear coat. Swirl marks, wash-induced marring, haze, light oxidation, certain water spots and other correctable imperfections can reduce clarity and gloss even when the vehicle is clean.

At Hangar One Preservation, the process starts with the condition of the paint—not a one-size-fits-all package. The vehicle is cleaned and decontaminated as required, the finish is inspected, and an appropriate correction process is selected before protection is applied.

01 / SWIRLS

Swirl & Marring Reduction

Machine correction can substantially improve circular wash marks, micro-marring and haze that reduce the clarity of the finish.

02 / CLARITY

Gloss & Paint Refinement

Polishing refines the clear coat to improve reflection, depth and overall finish quality rather than simply covering defects temporarily.

03 / PREPARATION

Paint Decontamination

Iron removal, chemical decontamination and clay treatment may be used when necessary to remove bonded contamination before machine polishing.

Paint correction FAQ

Before we correct the paint.

Does paint correction remove every scratch?

No. Correction is limited by the location and depth of the defect and by preserving a safe amount of clear coat. Deep scratches that extend beyond safely correctable paint may remain.

Do you clay bar the vehicle before paint correction?

Clay treatment is used when the paint condition requires mechanical decontamination. Chemical decontamination and iron removal may also be part of preparation depending on the vehicle.

Should I ceramic coat after paint correction?

It is an excellent time to consider ceramic protection because the finish has already been properly prepared and refined. The appropriate protection depends on your goals, vehicle use and maintenance expectations.
