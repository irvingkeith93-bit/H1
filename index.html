The Hangar One Standard

Hangar One Preservation delivers discreet, exacting care for luxury automobiles, private aircraft and marine vessels. Every surface is treated with the same respect its owner has for the machine itself.

“Preserving a fine automobile, aircraft or vessel is more than a service. It is the privilege of caring for a work of art in motion.”

We take pride in the work because we understand the feeling behind ownership. The engineering, craftsmanship and character of these machines deserve more than routine cleaning. They deserve patient inspection, disciplined technique and a preservation plan designed around how the asset is used, stored and enjoyed.
