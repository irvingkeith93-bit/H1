Paint decontamination

Washing removes dirt. Decontamination removes what washing leaves behind.

Even after a careful wash, automotive paint can still hold bonded contamination such as embedded iron particles, road fallout, industrial residue and other material that makes the surface feel rough or interferes with polishing and protection.

Hangar One Preservation uses a condition-based combination of chemical and mechanical decontamination to prepare the surface properly before paint correction, ceramic coating or other exterior preservation work.

01 / CHEMICAL

Iron Removal

A dedicated iron-removal step helps dissolve ferrous contamination embedded in the paint and wheels that normal washing may not remove.

02 / MECHANICAL

Clay Treatment

When bonded contamination remains, clay or another appropriate mechanical decontamination method may be used to refine the surface further.

03 / PREPARATION

Surface Evaluation

The paint is reassessed after decontamination so the next step—maintenance, correction or protection—is based on the actual condition of the finish.

Important

Clay treatment is not automatically used on every vehicle.

Mechanical decontamination can introduce light marring on some finishes. We use it when the condition of the paint calls for it, rather than treating clay as a mandatory step on every vehicle.

The H1 decontamination process

Proper preparation before correction or protection.

Paint decontamination FAQ

Common questions.

What is paint decontamination?

Paint decontamination removes bonded contaminants that remain after washing. Depending on the vehicle, that can include chemical iron removal and mechanical clay treatment.

How do I know if my paint needs clay treatment?

If the paint still feels rough after washing or has bonded contamination that chemical cleaning does not fully remove, mechanical decontamination may be appropriate. We assess the surface first rather than automatically claying every vehicle.

Should paint be decontaminated before ceramic coating?

Proper surface preparation is important before ceramic coating. The exact preparation depends on the condition of the vehicle and may include washing, chemical decontamination, clay treatment and paint correction.
